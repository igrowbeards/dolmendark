# Man at Arms

Weapons: Any

Armor: Any

Hit Points: 1D8 Per Level

Weapon Mastery: Choose one weapon type, you gain +1 to attacks and damage with that type, additionally add half your level rounded down to these rolls.

Grit: Choose strength or dexterity, You have advantage on checks of that type to overcome an opposing force, such as kicking open a stuck door (Strength) or slipping free of rusty chains (Dexterity).

Professional Specialisations: You start play with one professional specialization.

### Class Talent Table

| 2d6   | Effect                                                  |

## Professional Specializations

1) Battle rage: When in melee, you can choose to enter a berserk rage that lasts until the end of combat. While in the rage, you gain a +2 to attack and damage, but suffer a –4 penalty to AP and are unable to flee.
2) Cleave: When in melee with multiple foes, if you strike a killing blow, you may immediately make another attack against a second adjacent foe. The second attack is made with a –2 modifier.
3) Defender: When you are in melee with a foe, any attack rolls the foe makes against characters other than you receive a –2 modifier.
4) Last stand: If you are reduced to 0 Hit Points, you can continue to act during their death timer.
5) Leader: Mercenaries or retainers under your command and within near gain a +1 bonus to morale or loyalty checks. All your allies within near gain a +2 bonus to any rolls to resist fear effects.
6) Main gauche: When fighting with a Small melee weapon in the off hand, you may choose each round to gain either +1 AP or a +1 to melee attacks.
