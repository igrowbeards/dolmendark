# Faery Glamours

Glamours are fae spell like abilities used by enchanters and other denizens of faery.
All known glamours can be cast once daily by default.

Awe
Duration: 3 rounds
Range: Near
A group of creatures whose total levels do not exceed the casters are forced to roll morale due to your unwordly presense. Those who fail flee from you for the duration.

Beguilment
Duration: 3 rounds
Range: Near
A target humanoid of level 2 or lower regards you as a friend for the duration.

Breathe of the wind 
Duration: 3 rounds
Range: Near
The caster conjures a stiff breeze lasting 3 rounds. During this time the caster can move in total silence.

Cloak of darkness
Duration: 3 rounds
Range: Self
The caster is obscured by shadows and effectively invisible to casual observation for 3 rounds.

Conjure Treats 
Duration: Instant
Range: Near
Conjure a person or creatures favorite treat. If the target consumes the treat any subsequent charisma checks by the caster against the create are made with advantage.
The food appears real in every way but does not actually nourish the consumer.

Dancing Flame
Duration: 3 rounds
Range: Near
You can cause a pre-existing flame to leave it's source and float through the air in a direction of your choosing. The flame burns out after 3 rounds.

Disguise Object
Duration: special
Range: Close
An item the caster touches changes to look like some other mundane object. This effect lasts until the glamour is cast again or until the disguised object is touched by someone other than the caster.

Fairy Dust
Duration: 3 rounds/1 round
Range: Near
Glittering motes fill the air revealing invisible objects or creatures. Can be concentrated on a single creature to blind and stun them for one round.

Flame Charm 
Duration: 3 rounds
Range: Self
Conjure flame from your hand. The conjured flame casts light in near and can be used to light flammable materials. 

Darkness Charm 
Duration: 3 rounds
Range: Near
One mundane light source ( torch, lantern, candle, small campfire) supernaturally dims and ceases to cast light.

Fool’s gold
Duration: 5 rounds
Range: close
The caster makes up to 20 coins appear to be of a different denomination.
The illusion will fool all mundane investigation for the duration.

Forgetting
Duration: Instant
Range: Near
The caster causes a mortal or animal to forget whatever happened last round.
Saving Throw: The subject must save charisma to resist the effects

Masquerade
Duration: Permanent until dismissed or death.
Range: Self

The caster can change their appearance to any generic humanoid. A specific individual cannot be replicated, only a generic every-person of the target ancestry.

Mirth and Malice
Duration: 1 Turn
Range: Near

The caster can sow a sense of jubilation or angry despondense.
A number of creature levels up to the characters own level are affected.

Seeming
Duration: Permanent until touched
Range: self

The caster can change their garb to anythign style can imagine.
This illusion is visual only. The illusion is dispelled if anyone touches the caster's garb.

Silver Tongue
Duration: 1 round
Range: self

The caster can communicate with any creature, including animals or monsters for 1 round.

Through the Keyhole
Duration: Instant
Range: close

The caster touches a keyhole and appears on the other side.
Only mundane doors can be bypassed in this manner.

Vanishing
Duration: 3 rounds
Range: near

The caster briefly disappears from the sight of a selected creature within range.
Attacking the target of this spell immediately dispels the effect.

Walk in Shadows
Duration: 1 Round
Range: The caster

The caster walks into a pre-existing pool of shadow and walks out of another pre-existing shadow within near.
