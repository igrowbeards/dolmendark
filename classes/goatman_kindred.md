# Goatman Kindred Class

Weapons: Any

Armor: Any

Hit Points: 1D8 Per Level

Thick Fur - +2 to AC

Horns - d4 damage natural weapon

Wild Eyed - Menacing or antagonistic charisma checks against sentients of your level or lower get +2

### Class Talent Table

| 2d6   | Effect                                                |
|-------|-------------------------------------------------------|
| 2     | Your horn damage grows to the next die type (max d8)  |
| 3-7   | +1 to attacks with your horns                         |
| 8-9   | +2 to Str or Chr                                      |
| 10-11 | +1 to wild eyed charisma checks                       |
| 12    | choose a talent or +2 points to distrubute to stats   |
