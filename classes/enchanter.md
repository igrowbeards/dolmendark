# Enchanter Class

Weapons: Dagger, Staff, Shortsword, Longsword, Spear

Armor: Leather, Chain

Hit Points: 1d6 per Level

Faery Magic:

Glamours: You have the ability to cast small fae charms called glamours. You begin play with one glamour.

Runes: You can use faery runes, you begin play with one known rune.

| 2d6   | Effect                                              |
|-------|-----------------------------------------------------|
| 2     | learn a new glamour                                 |
| 3-7   | +1 to CHR or DEX           |
| 8-9   | Gain an extra daily casting of a glamour you know   | 
| 10-11 | choose a talent or +2 points to distribute to stats |
| 12    | learn a new rune of a level you can cast            |
