# Hunter Class

Weapons: Longbow, shortbow, crossbow, shortsword, dagger, spear

Armor: Leather Armor, Shields

Hit Points: 1d6 per Level

Woodsman - You roll checks to find and follow trails with advantage. Your party rolls with advantage when rolling to see if they get lost.

Hunter - You have advantage on checks to track, stalk, and sneak up on game animals. Your attacks against unaware game animals do an extra die of weapon damage.

Trapper - You have advantage on checks to set or disarm snares, dead falls, and other traps in the wild. 

Animal Companion - You form a bond with a single creature of animal level intelligence that is equal to or lower than your level. Once bonded, the creature  will follow basic commands and will fight to protect you. You may attempt to bond with one creature one time per day. Aggressive creatures may attack if the bonding attempt is unsuccessful.
