### Mossling Knacks

## Bird Tongue

1) Bird speech: You can speek to birds and understand their responses

2) Bird friend: You can charm a bird companion of Level 1 or less. If the bird fails an easy charisma check, it remains in your company until dismissed. It is a clever but mundane bird. It will follow simple commands to the best of it's ability. Only one bird may be charmed in this way at a time.

3) Tweet: Forest birds will relay a message of up to 10 words for you. When the message arrives to it's target it is reproduced in cacophonous tweeting mimicing the original phrase. The birds may inadvertantly change the message telephone game style if in transit over long distances.

4) Summon flock You can call upon the aid of woodland birds. A flock of small birds native to the region are summoned and remain in your service for 1d4 Turns.

## Lock Tongue

1) Open simple locks: You sing to a mundane lock for 1d4 rounds after which it opens.
2) Locate key: You sing to a lock for 1d4 rounds after which it whispers back general directions to it's nearest key. Only you can understand the locks reply.
3) Snap shut: Simple, mundane locks within near instantly snap shut after a single round of song.
4) Open any lock: 1d4 rounds of singing can persuade locks of any complexity to open, even magical locks. You must pass a normal charisma check, failure means the lock has refused your request. Critical fails against magical locks backfire and seal your mouth shut for 1d4 days.

### Root Tongue 
1) Root empathy - Once per day, you can whisper to one living plant root and ask about its surroundings. The root answers truthfully with at most 1d6 words.
2) Tuber feast: You can summon edible roots to the surface once per day. 1d4 fresh rations are produced.
3) Root rest: You may shelter in the roots of a tree or other large plant once per day. You are taken gently underground into the plant’s root system, where you may lie unnoticed for up to an hour, or overnight if asleep.
4) Summon Root beast: You can summon a monstrous root vegetable from the earth once per day. The creature arrives at the character’s location in 1d6 Rounds and obeys their commands for 1d6 rounds before fleeing.

### Thread Tongue

1) Thread persuasion: Your whistling can tie, untie, or unravel any textile thinner than rope. Doing so requires you to softly sing to the target for 1d4 rounds.
2) Direct cords: You commands loose strings to move as your direct. The threads may be commanded to move up to close per Round for as long as you whistle. Cords controlled in this way can slowly drag small objects, but are too weak to cause any damage to most living creatures directly.
3) Rope mastery: Ropes loosen or tighten at the your whistle.
4) Animate rope: At your command a rope animates to attack and entangle a foe. You must concentrate and continue to whistle while commanding a rope — entangled targets may move at half speed but may not take any other actions.

### Wood Tongue

1) Sense history: After 1d4 rounds of singing you learn the name of a wooden item’s creator or the name of the last person to touch it.
2) Sense emotions: You sing a quiet song to a piece of wood. After 1d4 rounds the wood shares the most recent strong emotion which the wood has absorbed from a sentient being.
3) Door whisperer: You can crossing your eyes and hum a tune while  peer at the surface of a wooden door or other barricade for 1 round, after which the woodgrain of the barricade subtly rearranges itself to show you the space on the other side. No other creatures can see or make sense of the minute changes.
4) Tree Friend: You can learn a tree’s true name. Once per day, the mossling may invoke one tree whose true name they know, gaining a brief glimpse of the tree’s surroundings. Invoking the true name of a deceased tree provokes a curious vision of the vegetable afterlife. Learning a tree's name takes 1d6 rounds of gentle song and conversation with the tree.

### Yeast Tongue
1) Ferment: Your saliva ferments sweet liquids at a rate of one pint per round. The liquid is transformed to a frothy, alcoholic brew, delicious to mosslings. There is a 2-in-6 chance of it being palatable to other folk.
2) Yeast Speech: You can commune with the yeasts in alcoholic beverages. Sipping from and gargling a drink which has been partly consumed by someone else reveals the person’s name.
3) Yeasty Burp: You can emit a yeasty belch once per day. The belch may be targeted at a creature of your level or lower within near , who must pass a hard consitition check or faint for 1d6 Rounds.
4) Brewers Feast: You are able to conjure a yeasty feast equivalent to 1d6 fresh rations. The food is composed of chunks of fleshy, compacted yeast, frothing sheets of slime, and bubbles of alcoholic brew.
