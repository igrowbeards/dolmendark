# Catfae Kindred Class

Weapons: Dagger, shortbow, shortsword, club
Armor: Leather, Mithral Chain
Hit Points: 1D4 Per Level

Nimble - Rolls to jump or climb are made with advantage. You roll any fall damage with disadvantage.

Chester: You can shapeshift into a large housecat at will. Doing so takes one round. The change lasts until dismissed which also takes one round. Rolls to move silently and undetected are rolled with advantage while in this form.

Wilder: Once per day you can shift into a shadowy cheshire cat form for 1d4 rounds. In this form you are nearly invisible except your glowing eyes and silvery teeth and claws. Attacks against you by creatures that can't see invisible are made at -2. Your AC is 12 + your dex modifier and you deal a d8 damage with your claw attack.
During the transformation you are blinded by rage. If all foes are vanquished before the duration ends you must make a hard charisma check each round or attack the nearest NPC or player character. 

Faery: You know one random fae glamour.
