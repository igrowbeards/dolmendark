# Moss Dwarf Kindred Class

Weapons: Club, crossbow, dagger, shortbow, shortsword
Armor: Leather, Chain, Pine Armor (cost and stat as plate, generally only available for purchase in moss dwarf settlements)
Hit Points: 1D6 Per Level

Hardy - You roll with advantage to resist any negative effects of non-sentient plants or mushrooms. Any positive effects work as well as possible.
Symbiosis - You may bond with a magical fungus or fungal creature and accept some of their spores into your own body to gain an effect of the fungus as determined by the GM. Removing a symbiosis requires magical healing like cure disease or a specially brewed potion.
Natural Camo - Checks to remain hidden are rolled with advantage when in the wild or in areas with large fungi.
Knack - You start play knowing one moss dwarf knack which can be used once a day.
